I’m just reading through [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/) today, and thinking about the new AirPods with camera, new HomePods, and an iPhone event in September.

I realised that I have never been more bored and uninterested in Apple’s latest products since I started using them at the age of 5.

A couple of years ago I insta-bought the new AirPods Pro as soon as they were available. Now, I mostly were my wired headphones, if any.

I haven’t worn my Apple Watch in months.

I adore my iPhone 13 mini – and I balk every time I pick up my wife’s giant, heavy iPhone 15 Pro Max (which is still much lighter than the 17 Pro Max).

I don't want a new iPhone (unless it's a [mini with USB-C](/posts/the-perfect-iphone/)). I don't want new AirPods. I am in the process of de-smartifying my smart home.

What's happening to me!?
